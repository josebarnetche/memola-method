CLIENT INTERACTION (30 points)
├── Communication Quality (10 pts)
├── Presentation Skills (10 pts)
└── Co-production Facilitation (10 pts)

PROCESS ADHERENCE (20 points)

## Assessment Scoring Guidelines

### Rating Scales

**5-Point Scale (Used in detailed rubrics):**
```
5 = Exceptional  - Significantly exceeds expectations, exemplary
4 = Strong       - Exceeds expectations, high quality
3 = Proficient   - Meets expectations, competent
2 = Developing   - Below expectations, needs improvement
1 = Insufficient - Significantly below expectations, not ready
```

**Point-Based Scoring:**
- Award points based on evidence against criteria
- Partial points allowed when appropriate
- Document rationale for scores
- Calibrate with other assessors

### Evidence Requirements

| Level | Primary Evidence | Supporting Evidence |
|-------|------------------|---------------------|
| Associate | Engagement deliverables, supervisor observation | Client feedback, tool outputs |
| Practitioner | Client outcomes, workshop recordings | Client NPS, peer feedback |
| Strategist | ROI documentation, case study | Team feedback, client interviews |
| Master | Portfolio, publications, mentee outcomes | Peer validation, community metrics |

## Assessor Guidelines

### Assessor Qualifications

| Assessment Level | Minimum Assessor Qualification |
|------------------|-------------------------------|
| Level 1 | Practitioner or higher |
| Level 2 | Strategist or higher |
| Level 3 | Master or external expert |
| Level 4 | Panel of 2+ Masters + external |

### Assessment Best Practices

```
BEFORE ASSESSMENT
□ Review all submitted materials thoroughly
□ Prepare questions based on evidence gaps
□ Review scoring criteria and calibrate
□ Ensure objectivity (no conflicts of interest)

DURING ASSESSMENT
□ Use structured evaluation forms
□ Document specific observations
□ Ask clarifying questions
□ Focus on evidence, not assumptions

AFTER ASSESSMENT
□ Complete scoring immediately
□ Provide written rationale for all scores
□ Identify development areas regardless of outcome
□ Maintain confidentiality
```

### Calibration Process

```
ASSESSOR CALIBRATION

ANNUAL CALIBRATION:
1. Review sample assessments together
2. Score independently, then compare
3. Discuss discrepancies
4. Agree on interpretation standards
5. Document calibration decisions

DRIFT PREVENTION:
• Quarterly review of scoring distributions
• Pair new assessors with experienced ones
• Feedback on assessment quality
• Address consistency issues promptly
```

## Appeals Process

### Grounds for Appeal

Appeals may be submitted for:
- Procedural errors in assessment
- Evidence not considered
- Assessor bias or conflict of interest
- Scoring errors

Appeals NOT accepted for:
- Disagreement with assessor judgment
- Comparison to other candidates
- Personal circumstances not disclosed

### Appeal Process

```
APPEAL TIMELINE

Day 1-14:    Submit written appeal with grounds
Day 15-21:   Appeal review committee assigned
Day 22-35:   Evidence review and deliberation
Day 36-42:   Decision communicated
Day 43-60:   Remediation or reassessment (if upheld)
```
