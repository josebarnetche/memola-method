# MEMOLA Practitioner Assessment Rubrics
## Level 2 Certification Evaluation Criteria

---

## Assessment Overview

**Certification Requirements:**
- Hold MEMOLA Associate Certification
- Complete 8-week advanced training program
- Pass 5 client engagements with positive outcomes
- Achieve minimum 75/100 total assessment score
- Demonstrate proficiency in Phases 1-4 (Measure, Evaluate, Map, Optimize)

**Assessment Components:**
| Component | Weight | Minimum Score |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| Technical Competency | 40% | 28/40 |
| Strategic Thinking | 30% | 21/30 |
| Facilitation & Leadership | 30% | 21/30 |
| **TOTAL** | **100%** | **75/100** |

**Additional Requirements:**
- Client NPS average ≥ 40 across engagements
- Positive outcomes documented for all 5 engagements
