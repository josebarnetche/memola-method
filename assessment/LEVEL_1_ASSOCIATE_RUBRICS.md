# MEMOLA Associate Assessment Rubrics
## Level 1 Certification Evaluation Criteria

---

## Assessment Overview

**Certification Requirements:**
- Complete 6-week training program
- Pass 3 supervised client engagements
- Achieve minimum 70/100 total assessment score
- Demonstrate proficiency in Phases 1-2 (Measure & Evaluate)

**Assessment Components:**
| Component | Weight | Minimum Score |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| Technical Competency | 50% | 35/50 |
| Client Interaction | 30% | 18/30 |
| Process Adherence | 20% | 12/20 |
| **TOTAL** | **100%** | **70/100** |
