# MEMOLA Strategist Assessment Rubrics
## Level 3 Certification Evaluation Criteria

---

## Assessment Overview

**Certification Requirements:**
- Hold MEMOLA Practitioner Certification
- Complete 10-week advanced training program
- Pass 7 client engagements demonstrating measurable ROI improvement
- Achieve minimum 80/100 total assessment score
- Demonstrate full method mastery (Phases 1-6)
- Successfully supervise at least 2 Associates

**Assessment Components:**
| Component | Weight | Minimum Score |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| Portfolio Review | 40% | 32/40 |
| Strategic Case Study | 30% | 24/30 |
| Team Leadership | 20% | 16/20 |
| Oral Examination | 10% | 8/10 |
| **TOTAL** | **100%** | **80/100** |
