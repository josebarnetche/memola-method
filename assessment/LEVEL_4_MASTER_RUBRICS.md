# MEMOLA Method Level 4: Master Recognition Rubrics
## Recognition-Based Assessment Framework

---

## Overview

Level 4 Master is a recognition-based distinction rather than a test-based certification. Candidates are evaluated through portfolio review, peer nomination, and demonstrated impact over an extended period (typically 12-24 months post-Strategist certification).

### Recognition vs. Certification

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    MASTER RECOGNITION FRAMEWORK                             │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                             │
│   TRADITIONAL CERTIFICATION          MASTER RECOGNITION                    │
│   ─────────────────────────          ──────────────────                    │
│   • Pass/fail assessment             • Continuous contribution review      │
│   • Point-in-time evaluation         • Cumulative impact assessment        │
│   • Individual demonstration         • Community impact emphasis           │
│   • Skills-focused                   • Leadership & legacy focused         │
│   • Examiner-driven                  • Peer-nominated & validated          │
│                                                                             │
│   RECOGNITION PILLARS                                                       │
│   ├── Method Innovation (25%)                                              │
│   ├── Mentorship Excellence (25%)                                          │
│   ├── Thought Leadership (25%)                                             │
│   └── Community Contribution (25%)                                         │
│                                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## Recognition Criteria Summary

| Pillar | Weight | Minimum Threshold | Evidence Required |
|--------|--------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Method Innovation | 25% | 2 accepted contributions | Innovation portfolio |
| Mentorship Excellence | 25% | 3 mentees certified | Mentee outcomes documentation |
| Thought Leadership | 25% | 3 external contributions | Publication/presentation portfolio |
| Community Contribution | 25% | Active participation | Community impact evidence |

**Overall Recognition Threshold:** Minimum "Strong" rating in at least 3 of 4 pillars, no pillar below "Developing"

## Pillar 1: Method Innovation (25%)

### Assessment Criteria

Innovation contributions are evaluated on originality, practical applicability, and adoption by the practitioner community.

#### Innovation Types

| Innovation Type | Description | Weight Multiplier |
|-----------------|-------------|-------------------|
| New Tool/Template | Creates reusable tool adopted by community | 1.0x |
| Process Improvement | Improves existing methodology step | 1.2x |
| New Framework | Develops new conceptual framework | 1.5x |
| Method Extension | Extends method to new industry/context | 1.5x |
| Foundational Enhancement | Improves core method principles | 2.0x |

## Master Recognition Process

### Nomination Requirements

Masters can be nominated by:
1. Self-nomination (with portfolio submission)
2. Peer nomination (by 2+ existing Masters)
3. Training Director nomination

### Recognition Timeline

```
MASTER RECOGNITION PROCESS TIMELINE

MONTH 1
├── Nomination submitted
├── Initial eligibility check
└── Portfolio request issued

MONTH 2
├── Portfolio submitted
├── Assigned to Recognition Panel
└── Panel review begins

MONTH 3
├── Panel deliberation
├── Additional information requested (if needed)
├── Peer validation (interview 2-3 references)
└── Final panel decision

MONTH 4
├── Decision communicated
├── Recognition ceremony (if approved)
├── Onboarding to Master community
└── If deferred: Development plan provided
```

### Recognition Panel Composition

```
RECOGNITION PANEL

COMPOSITION (5 members):
├── 2 existing Masters
├── 1 Training Director
├── 1 external industry expert
└── 1 client representative (from candidate's portfolio)

DECISION RULES:
├── Unanimous or 4-1 majority required for recognition
├── 3-2 results in deferral with development plan
├── Panel must document decision rationale
└── Candidate may appeal within 30 days
```

```
│  PILLAR RATING COMBINATIONS → DECISION                                      │
│                                                                             │
│  4+ pillars at Strong(4)+                    → RECOGNIZE                    │
│  3 pillars at Strong(4)+, 1 at Proficient(3) → RECOGNIZE                    │
│  3 pillars at Strong(4)+, 1 at Developing(2) → RECOGNIZE (conditional)      │
│  2 pillars at Strong(4)+, 2 at Proficient(3) → DEFER (development plan)     │
│  Any pillar at Insufficient(1)               → DEFER (significant gap)      │
│  2+ pillars at Developing(2) or below        → NOT RECOGNIZED               │
│                                                                             │
│  SPECIAL CONSIDERATIONS:                                                    │
│  ├── Exceptional(5) in 2+ pillars can offset 1 Developing(2)               │
│  ├── Time in role may be considered for borderline cases                   │
│  ├── Unique circumstances documented in rationale                          │
│  └── Appeals reviewed by separate panel                                    │
│                                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Post-Recognition Expectations

### Master Responsibilities

Once recognized, Masters are expected to maintain active contribution:

```
ONGOING MASTER EXPECTATIONS

ANNUAL MINIMUMS:
├── Mentor at least 1 new practitioner
├── Contribute 1+ innovation or improvement
├── 2+ thought leadership activities
├── 10+ community contribution points
└── Participate in 2+ recognition panels

STATUS MAINTENANCE:
├── Annual activity review
├── 3-year comprehensive review
├── Status can be moved to "Emeritus" if inactive
└── Emeritus Masters retain title but not active privileges
```

## Appendix: Reference Validators

### Peer Validation Questions

When contacting references (mentees, clients, peers), use these questions:

**For Mentees:**
```
1. How did this mentor support your development?
2. What specific skills did you develop through this mentorship?
3. How accessible and responsive was your mentor?
4. What would you say is this mentor's greatest strength?
5. Would you recommend this mentor to others? Why?
```

**For Clients:**
```
1. How would you describe working with this practitioner?
2. What impact did their work have on your marketing outcomes?
3. How did they handle challenges or disagreements?
4. What distinguishes them from other consultants you've worked with?
5. Would you engage them again? Would you refer them?
```

**For Peers:**
```
1. How does this practitioner contribute to the community?
2. What innovations or improvements have they made to the method?
3. How do they support other practitioners?
4. What is their reputation in the community?
5. Do you consider them worthy of Master recognition? Why?
```
