### Assessment Components by Level

```
LEVEL 1: ASSOCIATE (100 points)
├── Technical Competency (50 pts)
│   ├── Marketing Spend Audit Execution (15)
│   ├── ROI Tracking Setup (10)
│   ├── Competitive Analysis (15)
│   └── Tools & Technology (10)
├── Client Interaction (30 pts)
│   ├── Communication Quality (10)
│   ├── Presentation Skills (10)
│   └── Co-production Facilitation (10)
└── Process Adherence (20 pts)
    ├── Methodology Compliance (10)
    └── Deliverable Quality (10)

LEVEL 2: PRACTITIONER (100 points)
├── Technical Competency (40 pts)
│   ├── Market Analysis Excellence (15)
│   ├── Strategy Development (15)
│   └── Phase 1-2 Mastery (10)
├── Strategic Thinking (30 pts)
│   ├── Insight Synthesis (10)
│   ├── Trade-off Navigation (10)
│   └── Implementation Realism (10)
└── Facilitation & Leadership (30 pts)
    ├── Workshop Facilitation (15)
    └── Stakeholder Management (15)

LEVEL 3: STRATEGIST (100 points)
├── Portfolio Review (40 pts)
│   ├── Client ROI Documentation (15)
│   ├── Client Satisfaction Evidence (10)
│   ├── Deliverable Quality (10)
│   └── Engagement Diversity (5)
├── Strategic Case Study (30 pts)
│   ├── Problem Definition (10)
│   ├── Approach & Methodology (10)
│   └── Outcomes & Learning (10)
├── Team Leadership (20 pts)
│   ├── Associate Supervision (10)
│   └── Knowledge Contribution (10)
└── Oral Examination (10 pts)
    ├── Scenario Response (5)
    └── Leadership Philosophy (5)

LEVEL 4: MASTER (4 Pillars, 25% each)
├── Method Innovation
│   └── Innovations accepted, adoption, impact
├── Mentorship Excellence
│   └── Mentees certified, performance, retention
├── Thought Leadership
│   └── Publications, presentations, visibility
└── Community Contribution
    └── Peer review, training, events, resources
```
