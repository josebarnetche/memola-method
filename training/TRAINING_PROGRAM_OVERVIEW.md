## Introduction

The MEMOLA Method Training Program provides a structured path for marketing professionals to develop expertise in serving business owners with measurable marketing consulting. Based on the servuction principles of Eiglier and Langeard, the program emphasizes client co-production and outcome-focused service delivery.

## Implementation Guide

### For Organizations Adopting MEMOLA

**Step 1: Assess Current Team**
- Evaluate existing marketing consulting capabilities
- Identify gaps relative to MEMOLA phases
- Determine appropriate entry points for team members

**Step 2: Establish Training Infrastructure**
- Designate internal trainers (ideally Strategist level or above)
- Set up supervised practice opportunities
- Create engagement tracking systems

**Step 3: Launch Associate Program**
- Enroll first cohort of trainees
- Pair with experienced supervisors
- Begin client engagement pipeline

**Step 4: Build Certification Pipeline**
- Track trainee progress
- Conduct assessments per certification requirements
- Celebrate and announce certifications

**Step 5: Develop Advanced Practitioners**
- Support Practitioner and Strategist development
- Create innovation contribution opportunities
- Build toward Master recognition path

## Core Principles Across All Levels

### The Servuction Foundation

All MEMOLA practitioners, regardless of level, operate on these principles:

1. **Client as Co-Producer** - The client actively participates in creating value
2. **Simultaneity** - Service is produced and consumed together
3. **Physical Support** - Tools, dashboards, and deliverables enable the service
4. **Contact Quality** - Every client interaction matters
5. **Outcome Focus** - Success is measured in client results, not activities

### The MEMOLA Promise

Every certified MEMOLA practitioner delivers on this promise:

> "You will understand exactly where your marketing money goes, how you compare to competitors, what share of the market you own, and what specific actions will grow your business. You will not receive vague advice—you will receive measurable outcomes."

## Quality Standards

### Deliverable Standards (All Levels)
- Data sources cited and verified
- Recommendations include implementation specifics
- Dashboards functional and maintainable
- Reports client-presentable

### Service Standards (All Levels)
- Response time: <24 hours
- Meeting preparation: Agenda 24 hours in advance
- Deliverable review: Internal QA before delivery
- Issue resolution: Same-day acknowledgment

### Outcome Standards (Practitioner+)
- ROI tracking demonstrates improvement
- Competitive positioning shows gains
- Market share metrics verifiable
- Strategy implementation >70% adoption

---

## Assessment Philosophy

MEMOLA certification is not a test—it is a demonstration of capability through real client work.

**Key Principles:**
- Learning happens through practice, not just study
- Supervised engagements build confidence and competence
- Assessment criteria are transparent and objective
- Feedback is continuous, not just at certification
- Growth mindset is expected at all levels
