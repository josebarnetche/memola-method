# MODULE 1: Foundations of Servuction in Marketing Services

## Week 1 | 8 hours

### 1.1 The Servuction Model

**Learning Content:**

The servuction model (Eiglier & Langeard, 1989) establishes that services are produced through the interaction of five elements:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    SERVICE PRODUCTION SYSTEM                 │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                             │
│    ┌──────────────┐         ┌──────────────┐               │
│    │   INTERNAL   │         │   PHYSICAL   │               │
│    │ ORGANIZATION │────────▶│   SUPPORT    │               │
│    │   (Back)     │         │  (Front)     │               │
│    └──────────────┘         └──────┬───────┘               │
│           │                        │                        │
│           │                        ▼                        │
│           │                 ┌──────────────┐               │
│           │                 │   CONTACT    │               │
│           └────────────────▶│  PERSONNEL   │               │
│                             └──────┬───────┘               │
│                                    │                        │
│                                    ▼                        │
│    ┌──────────────┐         ┌──────────────┐               │
│    │    OTHER     │────────▶│   SERVICE    │◀──────┐       │
│    │   CLIENTS    │         │   OUTPUT     │       │       │
│    └──────────────┘         └──────────────┘       │       │
│                                    ▲               │       │
│                                    │               │       │
│                             ┌──────┴───────┐       │       │
│                             │    CLIENT    │───────┘       │
│                             │ (Co-Producer)│               │
│                             └──────────────┘               │
│                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Key Principles:**

1. **Simultaneity** - Service is produced and consumed at the same time
2. **Client Participation** - The client is essential to service creation
3. **Intangibility** - The core output cannot be stored or inventoried
4. **Heterogeneity** - Each service instance is unique
5. **Perishability** - Unused capacity cannot be recovered

**The Associate's Commitment:**

As a MEMOLA Associate, you commit to:

- **Accuracy** - Never fabricate or manipulate data
- **Transparency** - Clearly communicate limitations and assumptions
- **Client Education** - Help clients understand, not just receive
- **Continuous Learning** - Acknowledge gaps and seek guidance
- **Confidentiality** - Protect client data and competitive intelligence

**Report Structure:**

```

============================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (1 page)
   - Total marketing spend (period)
   - Key findings (3-5 bullets)
   - Critical recommendations (3 bullets)

2. SPEND OVERVIEW (2-3 pages)
   - Total spend by category (pie chart)
   - Monthly spend trend (line chart)

3. CHANNEL DEEP DIVE (1 page per major channel)

   - Efficiency ratios
   - Observations and concerns

4. WASTE IDENTIFICATION (1-2 pages)
   - Underperforming spend areas
   - Redundant tools or services
   - Misallocated budget
   - Quantified waste estimate

5. OPPORTUNITY ANALYSIS (1-2 pages)
   - Underfunded high-performers
   - Missing channels
   - Optimization potential

6. RECOMMENDATIONS (1 page)
   - Immediate actions (30 days)
   - Short-term adjustments (90 days)
   - Strategic considerations (6-12 months)

7. APPENDIX
   - Detailed data tables
   - Methodology notes
   - Data source documentation
```

# MODULE 3: ROI Tracking and Attribution

## Week 3 | 10 hours

### 3.1 Understanding Marketing ROI

**The ROI Formula:**

```
                    (Revenue Attributed to Marketing) - (Marketing Cost)
Marketing ROI (%) = ─────────────────────────────────────────────────── × 100
                                    Marketing Cost


Example:
Revenue Attributed: $500,000
Marketing Cost: $100,000

ROI = ($500,000 - $100,000) / $100,000 × 100 = 400%
```

**Alternative Metrics:**

| Metric | Formula | Use Case |
|--------|---------|----------|
| ROAS | Revenue / Ad Spend | Paid media evaluation |
| MER | Revenue / Total Marketing Spend | Blended efficiency |
| CAC | Total Marketing Spend / New Customers | Customer economics |
| CLV:CAC | Customer Lifetime Value / CAC | Investment payback |

### 3.2 Attribution Models

**Model Overview:**

```
Customer Journey: Ad Click → Email Open → Social Visit → Direct Purchase

FIRST-TOUCH ATTRIBUTION
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Ad Click     Email Open    Social Visit   Direct Purchase │
│    100%          0%             0%              0%         │
│     ★                                                       │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

LAST-TOUCH ATTRIBUTION
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Ad Click     Email Open    Social Visit   Direct Purchase │
│     0%           0%             0%             100%        │
│                                                  ★         │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

LINEAR ATTRIBUTION
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Ad Click     Email Open    Social Visit   Direct Purchase │
│    25%          25%            25%             25%         │
│     ★            ★              ★               ★          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

TIME-DECAY ATTRIBUTION
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Ad Click     Email Open    Social Visit   Direct Purchase │
│    10%          20%            30%             40%         │
│     ·            ·              ★               ★★         │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

POSITION-BASED (U-SHAPED)
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Ad Click     Email Open    Social Visit   Direct Purchase │
│    40%          10%            10%             40%         │
│    ★★            ·              ·              ★★          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Model Selection Guide:**

| Business Type | Recommended Model | Rationale |
|---------------|-------------------|-----------|
| Short sales cycle (<7 days) | Last-touch | Single interaction often drives conversion |
| Long sales cycle (>30 days) | Position-based or Linear | Multiple touchpoints matter |
| Brand-focused | First-touch | Initial awareness is valued |
| Performance-focused | Time-decay | Recent interactions weighted |
| Unknown/Starting | Linear | Fair starting point, adjust with data |

### 3.3 Implementing Tracking Infrastructure

**UTM Parameter System:**

```
https://example.com/landing-page?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=spring_sale&utm_content=ad_v1&utm_term=running+shoes

utm_source   = Traffic source (google, facebook, newsletter)
utm_medium   = Marketing medium (cpc, email, social, organic)
utm_campaign = Campaign name (spring_sale, brand_awareness)
utm_content  = Ad/content variant (ad_v1, banner_a)
utm_term     = Keyword (for paid search)
```

**UTM Naming Convention (MEMOLA Standard):**

```
Source:   lowercase, no spaces (google, meta, linkedin)
Medium:   standardized list (cpc, cpm, email, social, referral, organic)
Campaign: lowercase_underscores_date (spring_sale_2024q1)
Content:  descriptive_variant (hero_image_a, text_only)
Term:     keyword as searched
```

### 4.3 Share of Voice Analysis

**Share of Voice (SOV) Definition:**

```
                         Brand's Marketing Presence
Share of Voice (%) = ─────────────────────────────────── × 100
                     Total Market Marketing Presence


Example (Social Media SOV):
Client mentions: 1,500
Competitor A mentions: 2,000
Competitor B mentions: 1,000
Competitor C mentions: 500
Total: 5,000

Client SOV = 1,500 / 5,000 × 100 = 30%
```

**SOV Calculation by Channel:**

| Channel | Metric | Calculation |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| Organic Search | Keyword visibility | Share of ranking positions for target keywords |
| Paid Search | Impression share | Platform-reported or estimated ad impressions |
| Social Media | Mentions + engagement | Brand mentions + interactions / total market |
| PR/Media | Article count | Brand articles / total industry coverage |
| Content | Indexed pages | Brand content / total market content on topic |

# MODULE 5: Client Communication and Presentation

## Week 5 | 8 hours

### 5.1 The Servuction Communication Model

**Principle:** The client is a co-producer, not an audience.

**Communication Shifts:**

| Traditional Approach | MEMOLA Approach |
|---------------------|-----------------|
| "Here's what we found" | "Here's what we discovered together" |
| "You should do this" | "Based on your goals, this approach aligns" |
| "The data shows..." | "Your data reveals..." |
| "We recommend..." | "Given what you've shared, we suggest exploring..." |
| Presenting TO the client | Presenting WITH the client |

### 5.2 Structuring Client Presentations

**The SCAR Framework:**

```
S - SITUATION (Where are we now?)
    - Current state summary
    - Key metrics baseline
    - Context and constraints

C - COMPLICATION (What's the challenge?)
    - Problems identified
    - Gaps discovered
    - Risks if unchanged

A - ANALYSIS (What does it mean?)
    - Data interpretation
    - Patterns and insights
    - Competitive context

R - RECOMMENDATION (What should we do?)
    - Prioritized actions
    - Expected outcomes
    - Next steps
```

### 5.3 Handling Difficult Conversations

**Scenario Response Framework:**

**Scenario 1: Bad News Delivery**
> "Your marketing ROI is currently negative. For every dollar spent, you're generating only $0.75 in attributable revenue."

**Response Approach:**
1. State facts clearly without softening
2. Acknowledge the impact
3. Immediately pivot to opportunity
4. Propose specific action

**Script:**
> "The audit reveals your current marketing ROI is -25%. I know that's not what you hoped to hear. The important thing is: now we know exactly where the inefficiency is. The data shows 60% of this loss comes from two channels. We can address this."

**Scenario 2: Client Disagrees with Data**
> "That can't be right. Our sales team says leads from Facebook are our best."

**Response Approach:**
1. Acknowledge their perspective
2. Show methodology transparently
3. Explore the discrepancy together
4. Find the truth collaboratively

**Script:**
> "Your sales team's insight is valuable—they're on the front lines. Let's look at the data together and see if we can reconcile the difference. It's possible there's a tracking gap, or that lead quality differs from lead volume. Either way, we'll figure it out."

### 5.4 Presentation Delivery Checklist

**Before the Meeting:**
```
□ Test all technology (screen share, audio, video)
□ Send agenda 24 hours in advance
□ Confirm attendees and their roles
□ Prepare printed executive summary (if in-person)
□ Review previous meeting notes
□ Anticipate questions and prepare answers
□ Have raw data accessible for live queries
□ Prepare "parking lot" for off-topic items
```

**During the Meeting:**
```
□ Start with agenda and time check
□ Confirm understanding of objectives
□ Present with pauses for questions
□ Check for understanding at section breaks
□ Document decisions and action items live
□ Assign owners and deadlines to actions
□ Summarize next steps before closing
□ Confirm next meeting date
```

## Appendix D: Glossary of Terms

**Attribution Model:** Method for assigning credit to marketing touchpoints in the customer journey.

**CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Total marketing spend divided by number of new customers acquired.

**Conversion Rate:** Percentage of visitors/leads who take a desired action.

**CPL (Cost Per Lead):** Marketing spend divided by number of leads generated.

**MER (Marketing Efficiency Ratio):** Total revenue divided by total marketing spend.

**ROAS (Return on Ad Spend):** Revenue generated divided by advertising cost.

**ROI (Return on Investment):** (Revenue - Cost) / Cost, expressed as percentage.

**Servuction:** The service production system; the organized framework for delivering services.

**Share of Voice (SOV):** A brand's visibility relative to total market visibility.

**UTM Parameters:** URL tags used to track marketing campaign performance.
