# MEMOLA Quick Reference

## Phase Summary

| Phase | Name | Duration | Key Deliverables |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| 1 | MEASURE | 2-3 weeks | Spend audit, ROI baseline, waste report |
| 2 | EVALUATE | 2-3 weeks | Competitor profiles, SOV analysis, positioning map |
| 3 | MAP | 2-3 weeks | TAM/SAM/SOM, market share, segment analysis |
| 4 | OPTIMIZE | 3-4 weeks | Strategic plan, budget allocation, roadmap |
| 5 | LEVERAGE | Ongoing | Competitive moats, differentiation execution |
| 6 | ACCELERATE | Ongoing | Growth systems, expansion playbooks |

## Key Metrics

### Phase 1: Spend Audit
```
ROAS = Revenue / Ad Spend
CAC = Total Marketing Cost / New Customers Acquired
LTV = Average Revenue per Customer × Customer Lifespan
LTV:CAC Ratio = Target 3:1 or higher
```

### Phase 2: Competitive Analysis
```
Share of Voice = Brand Mentions / Total Category Mentions
Competitive Index = (Our SOV / Market Share) vs (Competitor SOV / Their Share)
```

### Phase 3: Market Sizing
```
TAM = Total market if 100% share
SAM = Portion we can realistically serve
SOM = Portion we can capture in 1-3 years
Market Share = Our Revenue / Total Market Revenue
```
