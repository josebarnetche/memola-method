# The MEMOLA Method
## A Servuction-Based Framework for Marketing Agency Service Delivery

---

## Overview

The **MEMOLA Method** is a structured approach for marketing agencies to deliver measurable value to business owners. Built on the servuction principles of Eiglier and Langeard (1989), it transforms marketing consulting from an intangible service into a systematic, co-created experience with quantifiable outcomes.

**MEMOLA** stands for:
- **M**easure
- **E**valuate
- **M**ap
- **O**ptimize
- **L**everage
- **A**ccelerate

---

## The Servuction Foundation

The Memola Method recognizes that marketing consulting is a **service production system** where:

1. **The Client (Business Owner)** is an active co-producer, not a passive recipient
2. **Physical Support** includes dashboards, reports, and analytical tools
3. **Contact Personnel** are the consultants who facilitate the process
4. **The Internal System** provides data infrastructure and methodologies
5. **Other Clients** contribute to benchmarking and industry insights

---

## The Six Phases of MEMOLA

### Phase 1: MEASURE (Marketing Spending Return)

**Objective:** Establish baseline metrics and ROI tracking systems

**Activities:**
- Audit current marketing expenditures across all channels
- Implement attribution modeling (first-touch, last-touch, multi-touch)
- Define Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) aligned with business goals
- Set up tracking infrastructure (UTMs, pixels, CRM integration)

**Deliverables:**
- Marketing Spend Audit Report
- KPI Dashboard Setup
- Attribution Model Selection Document
- ROI Baseline Report

**Client Co-Production:**
- Provide access to financial records and marketing invoices
- Define what "success" means for their business
- Validate KPI relevance to business objectives

**Skill Acquisition Checkpoint:**
*The practitioner can independently conduct a marketing spend audit and establish ROI tracking within 2 client engagements.*

---

### Phase 2: EVALUATE (Competition Analysis)

**Objective:** Map the competitive landscape and identify positioning opportunities

**Activities:**
- Identify direct, indirect, and aspirational competitors
- Analyze competitor marketing channels and messaging
- Assess competitor share of voice (SOV) across platforms
- Evaluate competitor pricing and value propositions
- Document competitor strengths, weaknesses, and blind spots

**Deliverables:**
- Competitive Landscape Matrix
- Share of Voice Report
- Competitor Messaging Analysis
- SWOT Analysis per Competitor
- Opportunity Gap Report

**Client Co-Production:**
- Identify competitors from their market perspective
- Share historical competitive intelligence
- Validate competitive positioning assumptions

**Skill Acquisition Checkpoint:**
*The practitioner can deliver a comprehensive competitive analysis that reveals actionable positioning opportunities within 3 client engagements.*

---

### Phase 3: MAP (Market Share Analysis)

**Objective:** Quantify current market position and growth potential

**Activities:**
- Define the Total Addressable Market (TAM)
- Calculate Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)
- Determine current Share of Market (SOM)
- Segment market by customer type, geography, and behavior
- Identify market share trends over time
- Benchmark against industry standards

**Deliverables:**
- Market Sizing Report
- Market Share Dashboard
- Customer Segmentation Matrix
- Market Trend Analysis
- Growth Opportunity Map

**Client Co-Production:**
- Provide sales data and customer information
- Validate market definitions and boundaries
- Contribute industry knowledge and relationships

**Skill Acquisition Checkpoint:**
*The practitioner can accurately size markets and calculate market share using multiple methodologies within 4 client engagements.*

---

### Phase 4: OPTIMIZE (Strategy Refinement)

**Objective:** Develop data-driven marketing strategy recommendations

**Activities:**
- Synthesize insights from Phases 1-3
- Identify highest-ROI marketing channels
- Develop customer acquisition cost (CAC) optimization strategies
- Create customer lifetime value (CLV) improvement plans
- Design marketing mix optimization models
- Establish budget allocation recommendations

**Deliverables:**
- Strategic Marketing Plan
- Channel Optimization Roadmap
- Budget Allocation Model
- CAC/CLV Improvement Framework
- Quick Win Implementation List

**Client Co-Production:**
- Participate in strategy workshops
- Provide operational constraints and capabilities
- Commit to implementation resources

**Skill Acquisition Checkpoint:**
*The practitioner can translate analytical insights into actionable strategic recommendations that clients can implement within 5 client engagements.*

---

### Phase 5: LEVERAGE (Competitive Advantage Activation)

**Objective:** Execute strategies that create sustainable competitive differentiation

**Activities:**
- Implement differentiation strategies identified in Phase 2
- Activate unique value propositions in market communications
- Build competitive moats through customer experience
- Develop content and thought leadership positioning
- Create strategic partnerships and alliances
- Establish early warning systems for competitive threats

**Deliverables:**
- Differentiation Activation Plan
- Brand Positioning Guidelines
- Competitive Response Playbook
- Partnership Strategy Document
- Competitive Intelligence System

**Client Co-Production:**
- Champion differentiation internally
- Allocate resources to strategic initiatives
- Provide feedback on market reception

**Skill Acquisition Checkpoint:**
*The practitioner can help clients achieve measurable competitive differentiation within 6 client engagements.*

---

### Phase 6: ACCELERATE (Continuous Growth)

**Objective:** Scale success and establish ongoing optimization cycles

**Activities:**
- Scale high-performing channels and campaigns
- Implement continuous testing and learning programs
- Establish regular review and optimization cadence
- Build internal client capabilities for sustainability
- Create growth forecasting models
- Design expansion strategies (new markets, segments, products)

**Deliverables:**
- Scaling Playbook
- Testing and Learning Framework
- Monthly/Quarterly Review Templates
- Capability Transfer Program
- Growth Forecast Model
- Expansion Strategy Document

**Client Co-Production:**
- Commit to ongoing optimization investment
- Build internal marketing capabilities
- Participate in regular review cycles

**Skill Acquisition Checkpoint:**
*The practitioner can establish sustainable growth systems that continue generating results after the engagement ends within 7 client engagements.*

## Skill Acquisition Framework

The Memola Method is designed to be acquired as a professional skill through progressive mastery:

### Level 1: MEMOLA Associate (Phases 1-2 Proficiency)
- Can conduct marketing spend audits
- Can perform competitive analysis
- Requires supervision on strategic recommendations
- **Certification:** Complete 3 supervised client engagements

### Level 2: MEMOLA Practitioner (Phases 1-4 Proficiency)
- Can independently lead measurement and analysis
- Can develop strategic recommendations
- Requires review on implementation planning
- **Certification:** Complete 5 client engagements with positive outcomes

### Level 3: MEMOLA Strategist (Phases 1-6 Proficiency)
- Full method mastery
- Can lead complex, multi-phase engagements
- Can train and supervise Associates
- **Certification:** Complete 7 client engagements demonstrating measurable ROI improvement

### Level 4: MEMOLA Master
- Innovation contributor to the method
- Can customize method for industry verticals
- Mentors Strategists and develops training materials
- **Certification:** Demonstrated thought leadership and method contribution

## Client Engagement Model

### Standard Engagement Structure

**Phase Duration:** 4-6 weeks per phase (24-36 weeks full method)

**Touchpoint Cadence:**
- Weekly progress calls (30 minutes)
- Bi-weekly working sessions (2 hours)
- Phase completion presentations (1 hour)
- Monthly executive summaries

**Client Commitments:**
- Designated point of contact with decision authority
- Data access and system credentials
- Timely feedback on deliverables (48-hour turnaround)
- Participation in scheduled sessions

### Modular Deployment

The Memola Method can be deployed in modules based on client needs:

| Module | Phases | Focus |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **MEMOLA Foundations** | 1-2 | ROI measurement + competitive intelligence |
| **MEMOLA Market Position** | 2-3 | Competitive analysis + market share |
| **MEMOLA Strategy** | 3-4 | Market position + strategic planning |
| **MEMOLA Growth** | 4-6 | Strategy through acceleration |
| **MEMOLA Complete** | 1-6 | Full transformation engagement |

## Quality Standards

### Deliverable Standards
- All analyses must cite data sources
- Recommendations must include implementation specifics
- Dashboards must update automatically or on defined schedule
- Reports must be client-presentable to their stakeholders

### Outcome Standards
- ROI tracking must demonstrate measurable improvement
- Competitive positioning must show differentiation gains
- Market share metrics must be verifiable
- Strategy implementation must achieve >70% adoption

### Service Standards
- Response time: <24 hours for client communications
- Meeting preparation: Agenda shared 24 hours in advance
- Deliverable review: Internal QA before client delivery
- Issue escalation: Same-day acknowledgment, 48-hour resolution plan
