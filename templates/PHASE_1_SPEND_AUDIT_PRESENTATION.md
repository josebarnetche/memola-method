## PRESENTATION CHECKLIST

**Before the Meeting:**
```
□ Data verified and calculations checked
□ Charts and visuals render correctly
□ Speaker notes reviewed
□ Backup data accessible for live questions
□ Printed executive summary (if in-person)
□ Technology tested (screen share, audio)
```

**Presentation Tips:**
```
□ Start with the headline, not the methodology
□ Tell the story of the data, don't just show it
□ Pause after key findings for reactions
□ Use "we discovered" not "I found"
□ Acknowledge good decisions, not just problems
□ End with clear, actionable next steps
```
